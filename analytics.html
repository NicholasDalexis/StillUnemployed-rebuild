              THEIR FEEDBACK

              Themes they like.

              Recorded “I like this theme” votes in the selected window. Counts are vote events, not unique voters.

                Each theme needs at least 5 distinct analytics visitors voting in this direction. Retries of the same event count once.

                ROOM TO IMPROVE

                Themes they dislike.

                Recorded “I don’t like this theme” votes in the selected window. This is separate from theme selections.

                  Each direction is withheld separately below 5 distinct analytics visitors. Missing groups are not zero votes; no approval rate is inferred.

                  AFTER AN APPLY CLICK

                  Time away, with context.

                  Mean observed away time for returned visits, limited to 15 minutes per observation.

                  This measures our tab becoming hidden and returning. It cannot tell what happened on the employer’s site. Time past the limit and missing returns remain uncertain.

                  ASK THE NOTEBOOK

                  What are you wondering?

                  Ask about totals, popular fields, roles, jobs, companies, theme votes or time on our legal pages. Answers use the selected window’s aggregates.

                  Try a suggested question to get started.

                  Questions stay in this browser. This is a limited aggregate lookup, with no demographic guesses or external AI service.

                  TIME ON OUR PAGES

                  Where they spend a moment.

                  Mean active time per measured page and session. Includes legal pages when enough visitors have opted in.

                    Active time is measured while this page is visible and the visitor is active. Timing events can repeat within a session; they are not page-view counts or proof someone read the text.

                    READ THE MARGINS

                    What these numbers can tell us.

                    People who opted in

                    Analytics include consented activity only. Ad blockers, offline sessions and missing events can leave gaps. These are not a count of everyone who visited.

                    Patterns, not identities

                    Interests describe activity on this board. They do not tell us a person’s age, background or qualifications.

                    Zeros need context

                    A zero means no recorded activity in this window. It does not prove an event is correctly wired. Unavailable measurements appear as N/A.

                    Inspect recorded event types

                    Recorded action counts, not unique people or a complete response history. Preference saves can include blank optional answers. These counts contain no typed answers or per-job not-fit details. Missing event types do not prove zero activity.
