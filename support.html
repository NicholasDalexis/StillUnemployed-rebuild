Help & support

StillUnemployed is operated by Elevate Media Technologies LLC.

For help with the board, sign-in, Saved, Tracker or your data, email Nic@jobhuntrecipe.com.

Account and data

Open Account settings from the board to sign in or manage your account. On iPhone, use the gear at the top left. When signed in, choose Delete account to begin deletion and confirm your identity. A pending or failed deletion can be resumed there. Your newsletter subscription is separate; unsubscribe through a newsletter email.

You can browse, save jobs and use Tracker without signing in. A device-only Saved or Tracker record remains local unless you sign in to sync it.

Listings and applications

Applications open on the employer’s website. Employers manage their hiring and application status. Use the listing’s availability reporting controls if a role is closed or its link no longer works.

Privacy choices

Use Privacy & recommendations to change optional analytics and activity-based recommendations. You can also remove Saved and Tracker items individually.
