# Board writing rules, 2.5.3

Recorded September 13, 2026 by Codex from Nic's approved feedback.

## Job summaries

Use three or four short bullets grounded in the employer posting. Lead with the concrete work, then useful collaborators or tools, then the day-to-day output. Start bullets with verbs. Avoid generic company praise, vague promises, copied paragraphs and repeated salary/location details already shown above. Do not invent requirements, qualification judgments, application status or closed/open evidence. A summary is a reading aid; the employer posting remains authoritative.

Example structure: Create campaign assets. Adapt them for social and web. Work with the brand and marketing teams. Keep files ready for production. These are illustrative, not facts to paste into unrelated jobs.

Keep Share, Save and Apply easy to find. Do not insert advice content into the job summary. Preserve the existing optional newsletter cadence.

## Advice notes

Prefer two short sentences or up to three concise bullets, about 45 words or fewer. Keep the central advice and any essential accuracy condition. Remove anecdotes or examples that duplicate the main point. An illustration should support the idea without creating a tall empty region.

## Typography and labels

Use local Lexend for text people read: role names, metadata, summaries, advice body, forms and legal text. Keep Archivo Black for intentional display headings and Indie Flower for handwritten labels. Lexend is Nic's readability preference; we make no clinical claim.

Our picks is the general sourcing label. Handpicked requires recorded individual review. Neither label establishes that a role is currently open. Maintain the same wording across Jobs, Internships, detail/share surfaces and policy explanations.
