Style guide

Warm paper, useful notes and a little handwriting. A reference for keeping the board readable and recognizably ours, from a phone to a wide screen.

01 Colors with a job to do

Use the shared palette. The warmer yellow comes from the original board’s high-salary card. Navigation and primary actions use that same paper treatment.

One yellow family. Don’t invent a separate yellow for each button.

05 Give the page some room

Use a small spacing scale, aligned edges and natural document flow. Let copy wrap and sections grow instead of shrinking an entire desktop composition.

Desktop

Main contentSupporting note
  • Center the homepage hero within a 1,500px maximum width.
  • Use two columns where the content benefits from sitting together.
  • Keep paragraph width comfortable, around 60–70 characters.
  • Use 24–48px between sections and 16–24px inside reading surfaces.

Phone

Main contentSupporting note
  • Stack related sections in reading order; preserve the important action.
  • Keep 16px side space where the viewport allows it.
  • Primary touch controls are at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels.
  • Check 320px width, long labels and enlarged text. Essential controls must remain visible.

The spacing scale

481216243248

4–8px within tight groups. 12–16px between related controls. 24–48px between bigger ideas.

Notes that open over the page

  • Default maximum width: 640px, with space at both viewport edges.
  • Limit height to the visible viewport, leaving about 24–32px outside the note.
  • Scroll long content inside the note and keep the close control reachable.
  • Support Escape, outside-tap dismissal, keyboard focus containment and focus return.

A clear close button remains available even when outside-tap dismissal is supported.

06 Make every state feel considered

When someone interacts

Hover
Change the shadow or underline. Keep the hit area stable and the label readable.
Focus
Use a clear, offset outline. Keep it visible above paper edges and outside clipped artwork.
Pressed
Reduce the shadow to suggest contact. Keep the action and its outcome clear.
Disabled
Use a native disabled control with legible text and a nearby reason. No hover invitation.

When something takes time

Loading
Say what is loading. Keep already available information useful.
Empty
Explain the empty result and provide a next step, such as clearing filters.
Error
Keep the person’s input. Explain what failed and provide a retry path.
Motion
Keep transitions brief and optional. Honor reduced-motion preferences.
