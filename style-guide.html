The anatomy stays simple.

  1. Context: a short “note to self” label, or a clear sponsorship disclosure.
  2. Hook: one thought that makes sense by itself. Aim for roughly 5 to 10 words, then let it wrap.
  3. Support: one short line or a small related drawing. Keep the margin, tape and ruling behind the words.
  4. Open prompt: continue the thought with a useful phrase. Give the whole summary a clear keyboard focus.
  5. Payoff: answer the hook in plain words, using a short list when it helps. Keep any next action below the answer.

These specimens use the loaded Indie Flower 400 face, 24px hooks, 20px prompts and the shared paper, ink, tape and rule tokens. The board’s existing advice notes open a dialog; these inline examples demonstrate the same content order without collecting an email.

Aim for roughly 85% of advice notes to include a substantial drawing related to the idea. Keep a few text-only notes for rhythm. A tiny arrow or theme decoration does not count as the illustration. Relevant internships, campus jobs and brand ambassador work deserve their own examples.

Keep the relationship visible: name the sponsor, or say when a product is ours. A promotional note keeps the notebook style and a clear destination. It never uses a salary band or a human-verification stamp.

06 A few drawn details

Small drawings give the notes personality. Keep them separate from the information someone needs to act on.

Use a small accent at a card edge. Keep names, salaries and actions clear. Drawings never stand in for a button label, salary band or verification stamp.

08 Give the page some room

Original keeps the shared layout. Use a small spacing scale, aligned edges and natural document flow. Let copy wrap and sections grow instead of shrinking an entire desktop composition.

Desktop

Main contentSupporting note
  • Center the homepage hero within a 1,500px maximum width.
  • Use two columns where the content benefits from sitting together.
  • Keep paragraph width comfortable, around 60–70 characters.
  • Use 24–48px between sections and 16–24px inside reading surfaces.

Phone

Main contentSupporting note
  • Stack related sections in reading order; preserve the important action.
  • Keep 16px side space where the viewport allows it.
  • Primary touch controls are at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels.
  • Check 320px width, long labels and enlarged text. Essential controls must remain visible.

The spacing scale

481216243248

4–8px within tight groups. 12–16px between related controls. 24–48px between bigger ideas.

Notes that open over the page

  • Default maximum width: 640px, with space at both viewport edges. The four-note welcome overview may use 820px.
  • Limit height to the visible viewport, leaving about 24–32px outside the note.
  • Scroll long content inside the note and keep a clear close or back action reachable.
  • Support Escape, outside-tap dismissal, keyboard focus containment and focus return.

Keep one clear way out: a close button on an overview, or a Back action inside a detail note. Outside-tap dismissal is an additional convenience.

09 Make every state feel considered

When someone interacts

Hover
Change the shadow or underline. Keep the hit area stable and the label readable.
Focus
Use a clear, offset outline with a light separation ring so it survives both dark and light paper. Keep it visible above paper edges and outside clipped artwork.
Pressed
Reduce the shadow to suggest contact. Keep the action and its outcome clear.
Disabled
Use a native disabled control with legible text and a nearby reason. No hover invitation.

When something takes time

Loading
Say what is loading. Keep already available information useful.
Empty
Explain the empty result and provide a next step, such as clearing filters.
Error
Keep the person’s input. Explain what failed and provide a retry path.
Motion
Keep transitions brief and optional. Honor reduced-motion preferences.

Read the ink with its paper

Ink colors belong to their surfaces. Check text contrast before reusing a color pair.

10 Sound like a person. Name the action.

Short, direct and useful.

Give someone one idea and a next step. Advice can be curious or playful; errors and account messages should be calm and specific.

Keep deliberate lowercase in short handwritten notes. Preserve company names, Google and product labels. Do not force every sentence into lowercase or add emphasis to every word.

Use real examples and supported claims. Do not invent a success story, a statistic or a promise of getting hired.

Let the label do the work.

Action
“Save job” names the result. “Saved” confirms it happened.
Recovery
Say what failed and what remains safe, then offer “Try again.”
Availability
Use “job no longer available.” Keep the next useful action close.
Opening
Continue the advice hook with a specific prompt. A control that leaves the page should name its destination.

11 Keep the name and the people recognizable

Photography has a reason.

Use the site’s approved photos and illustrations where they explain a person, role or story. Keep natural proportions, place captions outside the image and check the crop on a phone.

Do not bake essential words into a picture. Give informative images meaningful alternative text; purely decorative images get empty alternative text.

The name needs breathing room.

StillUnemployed.com

Keep the full spelling and the established lettering for its location. This guide uses handwriting; the homepage keeps its serif title and the board keeps its heavy display heading.

Leave clear space around the name. Do not stretch it, tuck tape across the letters or turn it into a different logo for each theme.

Use the full name in accessible link text. Employer logos and third-party marks identify their owners; they are not decorative theme assets or an endorsement.
